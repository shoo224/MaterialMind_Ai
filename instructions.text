Milestone 2 Evaluation Criteria (12 Marks)

* Review of Existing Systems and Project Feasibility– 3 Marks  
* Objectives and Methodology of the Proposed Work– 3 Marks  
* Relevance of Algorithms / Techniques – 3 Marks  
* Synchronization of Project Design & Implementation– 3 Marks  

Total: 12 Marks


 Report Submission

The report submitted for Milestone 2 must be a combined report covering the criteria of both Milestone 1 (M1) and Milestone 2 (M2).


Milestone 1 criterion: 
* Problem Domain Identification and Analysis – 3 Marks

Milestone 2 criteria:  
* Review of Existing Systems and Project Feasibility  
* Objectives and Methodology of the Proposed Work  
* Relevance of Algorithms / Techniques  
* Synchronization of Project Design & Implementation

Additionally:
     • Only one member from each team should submit the report on behalf of                         the entire team. Multiple submissions from the same team are not required.

* At least a partial demonstration of the project implementation must be presented during the evaluation.
